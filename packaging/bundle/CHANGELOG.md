# @friday/bundle

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- FRIDAY can be installed onto your Mac, and she starts when you log in.

  You install her, run `friday init` once to create her rules and her keys, and tell her to start at
  login. From then on she is running when you are, and `friday verify` will tell you the record of what
  she has done is intact.

  Two things she now says that she did not say before. She writes down that she started, before she
  does anything else — so the log begins with her arrival rather than with the first thing she checked.
  And when she will not start because she has not been set up on a machine, she names the command that
  fixes it instead of only naming the file she could not find.
