# Changelog

User-visible history of FRIDAY, written in plain language for someone who does not read code.

## 0.1.0 — Milestone 4, Installable

**FRIDAY can be installed onto your Mac, and she starts when you log in.**

You install her, run `friday init` once to create her rules and her keys, and tell her to start at
login. From then on she is running when you are, and `friday verify` will tell you the record of what
she has done is intact.

Two things she now says that she did not say before. She writes down that she started, before she
does anything else — so the log begins with her arrival rather than with the first thing she checked.
And when she will not start because she has not been set up on a machine, she names the command that
fixes it instead of only naming the file she could not find.

**She still does not think, and she is not connected to anything.** She records, she can be told no,
her decisions are written down, and now she runs on your machine. What she does *for* you begins at
Milestone 6.

Nothing in this release was proposed by FRIDAY. She does not have that department yet.

## How this file is maintained

Entries are generated by **Changesets** and assembled during the release process
([Chapter 27](docs/01-bible/27-cicd-pipeline.md)). It was adopted at Milestone 4 under
[ADR-0036 §6](docs/adr/0036-packaging-delivers-friday-init-provisions.md), which asked for approval
before adding it rather than adding it and mentioning it afterward.

`packaging/bundle` is the versioned unit; every other package in the workspace stays at `0.0.0`
because nothing is published to a registry and per-package versions would be fiction. The mechanical
per-version record lives in [`packaging/bundle/CHANGELOG.md`](packaging/bundle/CHANGELOG.md); this
file is the one written for you.

Two rules for entries, whenever they start:

1. **Written for the owner, not for a programmer.** "Gmail now waits longer before retrying, because
   it was giving up too early on large attachments" — not "refactored the connector retry layer."
2. **Changes FRIDAY proposed herself are marked as such.** Provenance stays visible permanently, the
   same way the `ai-authored` label does on pull requests.
